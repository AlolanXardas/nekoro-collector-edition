# Nekoro Collector Edition

Collect the blocks you find, copy the ones you need and keep your items in bags.

Made for my [Nekoro Pokemon](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/nekoropolia) modpack, but it works with any mods. Required on the client and the server.

## Features

- **Collector Table:** put a block in to add it to your collection (you keep it), convert blocks into points and take copies of anything you collected. Every player has their own collection.
- **Collector Catalog:** opens the same window from anywhere.
- **Bags:** 120 bags that work like ender chests or keep their own items, with up to 256 slots and automatic pickup.
- What can be collected, the price of a copy and every bag are set in `config/Nekoro/CollectorEdition/`.

## Building

Needs JDK 21. `source/build-all.ps1` builds NeoForge and Fabric for Minecraft 1.21.1 into `Gotowe/<version>`.
