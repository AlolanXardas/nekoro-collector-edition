Collect the blocks you find, copy the ones you need and keep your items in bags.
Made for my Nekoro Pokemon modpack, but it works with any mods.

Required on the client and the server.

Work in progress

All my projects are being prepared for a big update of my modpack. I'm changing them step by step so they fit together, and they should be ready by the end of this year.
Many graphics are temporary until the mechanics of all my mods are finished.

Found a bug, have an idea, or need this mod on another loader or Minecraft version?
Tell me in the comments or on Discord.

Features

Collector Table

  • Put a block into the table to add it to your collection. You keep the block.
  • Convert blocks into points. By default one copy costs 10 converted blocks.
  • Click a block in your collection to take copies. A button sets how many one click takes: 1, 8, 16, 32 or 64 by default.
  • Every player has their own collection and points.

Browsing the collection

  • Search by name, and move between categories with the bar above the blocks.
  • Group the categories like the creative tabs, by shape or by material.
  • Sort by category, name, mod or newest.
  • Mark blocks as favorites or hide them.
  • Everything that can be collected is shown, what you don't have yet is darkened. A button shows only what you collected.

What can be collected

  • Full blocks, slabs, stairs, walls, fences, doors, trapdoors, panes, carpets, buttons and pressure plates.
  • Blocks are recognised by what they are in the game, not by their name, so blocks of other mods work without any setup.
  • All blocks of Macaw's mods, and their wood variants from Every Compat, can be collected too.
  • Ores, food, plants, work tables, redstone parts and blocks with a use are left out.
  • Every list can be changed in the config.

Bags

  • 120 bags with the same ids and names as the coins of Nekoro Coins: 40 colours, 40 with a picture and 40 with a cat.
  • By default a bag works like an ender chest: every player has one storage for each bag, shared by all bags with that id.
  • A bag can also keep its own items instead, set in the config.
  • 54 slots, 256 with scrolling in Netherite bags. Any bag can have from 1 to 256 slots in the config.
  • Pickup button: items you pick up go straight into the bag. Off, only what the bag already holds, or everything.
  • A bag can be limited to chosen items in the config.
  • Crafted from an ender chest, leather and 1, 2 or 3 of a material: 1 for the colour, 2 for the picture, 3 for the cat.
  • The sort and transfer buttons of Nekoro Quality of Life work on the whole bag.

Other features

  • Collector Catalog: Opens the Collector Table window from anywhere with a right click.

Config

  • All files are in config/Nekoro/CollectorEdition/ and every option is explained in the file.
  • collection.json: the price of a copy, the amounts per click, showing what is not collected.
  • allowed.json and blocked.json: what can be collected. Blocked always wins.
  • bags.json: name, recipe, slots, allowed items and storage of every bag, and turning a bag off.
  • On a server the server's files count.
